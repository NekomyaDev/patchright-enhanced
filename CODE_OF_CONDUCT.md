# Code of conduct

Everyone participating in patchright-enhanced issues, pull requests, discussions,
and other project spaces is expected to:

- Be respectful and welcoming, including toward newcomers and differing experience levels.
- Give specific, constructive feedback; discuss the work without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy and consent. Do not publish another person's private information.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, and spam.

These expectations apply to maintainers and contributors alike.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report misconduct privately using a contact method a repository maintainer has
published on their GitHub profile. Do not post private evidence in public issues.
If no private maintainer contact is available, use GitHub's reporting tools for
platform abuse. For vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, issue a warning, or temporarily or
permanently restrict participation according to the severity and repetition of
an incident. Reports should be handled discreetly, sharing details only as needed
to investigate and respond. A maintainer involved in a report should defer review
to another maintainer where one is available.
